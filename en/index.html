About Me

UX Designer with
a Business Mindset

I'm a UX Designer with a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from UNIFIO and 2.5 years of experience at TOTVS, where I worked on modernizing complex systems, researching with users with special needs, and building component libraries. I develop in JavaScript, Java, and Flutter, and use applied AI with Claude Code and N8N — which lets me go from concept to implementation without depending on another team.

Beyond product and design, I've also worked as a performance marketing professional: I managed over US$280K in paid media for third-party clients and my own ventures, and sold over US$19K in physical products and digital products worldwide. I ran all of it independently — product development, marketing, copywriting, and support — which gives me a rare perspective: I design thinking about conversion, retention, and real impact, not just pretty interfaces.
